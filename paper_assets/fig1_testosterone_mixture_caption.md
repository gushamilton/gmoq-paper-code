# fig1_testosterone_mixture

Figure 1. Reading a sex-blind quantile GWAS through a two-component mixture model. Testosterone, UK Biobank; n = 399,710 (199,572 female, 200,138 male). **a**, Distribution of the analysed phenotype in the unshrunk trait and in three arms in which the male-female separation is shrunk by 0.2, 0.5 and 0.7, increasing the overlap. The black line is the whole-cohort distribution seen by the sex-blind Stage 1 analysis; shaded areas are its female and male parts, each scaled by its share. **b**, Stage 2 weights, the derivative of each quantile with respect to each parameter, for the fitted two-component skew-normal mixture: female location, male location and membership gamma, the log odds of belonging to the upper (male) component. Solid lines use the model-density weights; dashed lines, in the unshrunk arm and at 0.2, are the hybrid weights used there, whose membership column is divided by the Stage 1 kernel density. Weights are shown for chromosome 1 and are identical on every chromosome. Stage 2 is ordinary least squares with the pooled Stage 1 covariance (Methods).

Source data: results/03_testosterone/recovery, results/02_mixtures/stage2_mixtures.
