# fig5_scale_invariance

Figure 5. Scale-invariant dimensionality separates scale-reducible, heterogeneous and mixture traits. UK Biobank lead variants; quantile profiles are converted across transformations directly from Stage 1 output (Methods). **a**, One-dimensional trait: IGF-1 (644 lead variants; n = 438,938) on log, untransformed and Box-Cox (lambda = 0.57) scales, each profile divided by its mean effect. Thin lines show the 120 most precise leads; heavy line and band show the pointwise median and interquartile range across all 644 leads; dashed line, pure shift. The median profile is approximately flat on the Box-Cox scale. **b**, Two-dimensional trait: GGT (454 lead variants; n = 441,089) on untransformed, log and Box-Cox (lambda = -0.67) scales. Grey lines show the 120 most precise leads and dashed grey line the pointwise median across all leads; coloured curves and bands show two high-precision leads (rs2650000 and rs12373325, 95% confidence intervals). Log transformation flattens rs2650000 while leaving rs12373325 steeply tilted; the trait-flattening Box-Cox transformation bends the two variants in opposite directions, showing that no monotonic transformation flattens both. **c**, Ratio of observed whitened principal-component variance share to its parallel-analysis null median for PC2 to PC5 (ticks, null 95th percentile; dashed line, 1) across untransformed, log and Box-Cox transformations for five traits. Symbols for all three transformations coincide identically (maximum difference < 10^-10), demonstrating exact scale invariance and distinguishing one-dimensional (IGF-1), two-dimensional (BMI, GGT, HbA1c without diabetes) and three-dimensional (HbA1c) genetic architectures. **d**, PC1 and PC2 scores from unit-length whitened profiles for the 472 log HbA1c leads (n = 441,144), coloured by association with diabetes (logistic regression in the same participants, two-sided Bonferroni P < 0.05/472; Pearson r = 0.81 between PC2 and diabetes z statistic). Exemplar diabetes loci (TCF7L2, HLA-DOB, CDKAL1) separate cleanly from a comparison locus (FN3KRP) matched on the absolute mean-effect z statistic purely from quantile-profile geometry. GGT, gamma-glutamyltransferase; IGF-1, insulin-like growth factor 1; PC, principal component.

Source data: results/01_data/baseline_quantiles, results/01_data/lead_profiles, results/06_traits/moments, results/06_traits/moments, results/07_hba1c/skew_diabetes.
