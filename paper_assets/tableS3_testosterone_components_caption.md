# tableS3_testosterone_components

Table S3. Testosterone mixture components against sex-stratified reference GWAS, by phenotype and mixture weight design. One row per phenotype, weight design and mixture parameter; n = 399,710 (199,572 female, 200,138 male). Stage 2 by ordinary least squares with the pooled Stage 1 covariance. Weights: ‘Model’, every column divided by the fitted mixture density; ‘Hybrid’, only the membership (γ) column divided by the Stage 1 kernel density (Supplementary Note 2). Design: ‘Reported’, the design used in Figs 1 and 2 and Supplementary Fig. S8 (hybrid for unshrunk and F = 0.2, model for F = 0.5 and 0.7); ‘Alternative’, the other design. Phenotype: unshrunk, or male values shifted towards the female mean by F = 0.2, 0.5 or 0.7 of the difference. Components: female (lower component location), male (upper component location) and membership (γ). GWS variants: two-sided P < 5 × 10⁻⁸. λGC: median genomic inflation of the component and of the reference. r and slope: Pearson correlation and least-squares slope against the reference over all variants and over reference GWS variants; LOCO-matched reference: fitted in the same participants with the same covariates and leave-one-chromosome-out predictions. IVW: inverse-variance weighted Mendelian randomization slope (s.e., number of instruments) in each direction. Membership is compared only with a GWAS of chromosomal sex in the same 399,710 participants (sign aligned to male = 1) and, in the last LDSC column, with a GWAS of chromosomal sex in all 462,869 genotyped participants; its other cells are blank. GWAS, genome-wide association study; GWS, genome-wide significant; IVW, inverse-variance weighted; LDSC, LD score regression; LOCO, leave-one-chromosome-out; rg, genetic correlation; s.e., standard error.

Source data: results/03_testosterone/recovery, results/03_testosterone/sex_gwas.
