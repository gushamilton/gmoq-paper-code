# figS11_hba1c_skew

Figure S11. Asymmetry beyond shift and spread in HbA1c lead variants against their association with diabetes. Each point is one of 472 lead variants for log HbA1c (HbA1c-increasing allele; n = 441,144 participants). The vertical axis is the z statistic for asymmetry beyond shift and spread, the third L-moment of the residual after a least-squares fit of shift and spread to the lead's quantile-effect profile (Methods); the horizontal axis is the z statistic for association with diabetes (logistic regression; 20,728 cases, 420,416 controls). Dashed lines mark the two-sided Bonferroni threshold for asymmetry (P = 0.05/472); Asymmetry, leads beyond it; circled and labelled points are the six leads shown in Fig. 5c. Of the 69 leads with asymmetry beyond shift and spread, 58 (84%) were associated with diabetes at two-sided P < 0.05/472, against 51 (13%) of the other 403; Spearman correlation of the two z statistics, ρ = 0.81. In a separate analysis of the 420,416 participants without diabetes, 4 of these 69 leads retained the asymmetry. Both tests are two-sided and Bonferroni-corrected for the number of leads. HbA1c, glycated haemoglobin.

Source data: results/07_hba1c/skew_diabetes.
