# fig2_testosterone_recovery

Figure 2. Recovery of sex-specific testosterone effects from a sex-blind analysis. n = 399,710; the sex-stratified reference is REGENIE in the same 199,572 female and 200,138 male participants. Hybrid membership weights are used in all arms (Methods). **a**, G-MoQ female and male estimates against the sex-stratified reference at clumped reference lead variants (two-sided P < 5 x 10-8), by degree of shrinkage; the dashed line is y = x. For no shrinkage, shrinkage 0.2, 0.5 and 0.7, respectively, female lead counts are 97, 96, 96, 97, Pearson correlations are 0.999, 0.999, 0.995, 0.949 and ordinary least-squares slopes are 1.03, 1.04, 1.09, 0.78; male lead counts are 111, 111, 111, 112, correlations are 0.999, 0.998, 0.994, 0.986 and slopes are 1.02, 1.00, 0.93, 0.89. The all-significant-SNP statistics in Supplementary Table S3 use 1,578 female and 3,048 male reference-significant SNPs before clumping. **b**, LD score regression genetic correlation of each G-MoQ component with its reference: sex-stratified GWAS for the female and male components, and a GWAS of chromosomal sex in the same participants for membership. **c**, Computational cost per additional variant on one core against sample size, for direct mixture maximum likelihood, G-MoQ Stages 1 and 2, and Stage 2 alone given the Stage 1 output. **d**,**e**, Two exemplar loci selected in advance: rs1260326, with effects on all three parameters, and rs7097461, with a male-specific effect. Left, per-allele estimates with 95% confidence intervals (location on log testosterone, membership on the log-odds scale); right, Stage 1 quantile effects (grey, 95% confidence intervals) with the fitted profile (black) and each parameter's contribution. Few participants lie between the modes, so effects near tau = 0.5 are very imprecise and values beyond the axis are drawn at its edge as triangles. Membership is oriented to the upper (male) component.
