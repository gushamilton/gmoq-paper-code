# fig2_testosterone_recovery

Figure 2. Recovery of sex-specific testosterone effects from a sex-blind analysis. n = 399,710; the sex-stratified reference is REGENIE in the same 199,572 female and 200,138 male participants. Stage 2 is the mixture model of Fig. 1, fitted by ordinary least squares with the pooled Stage 1 covariance. **a**, G-MoQ female and male estimates against the sex-stratified reference at reference lead variants (two-sided P < 5 x 10-8), by degree of shrinkage; r is the Pearson correlation and the slope is from ordinary least squares, both over the plotted leads; the dashed line is y = x; outlined panels use hybrid weights (Methods). **b**, LD score regression genetic correlation of each G-MoQ component with its reference: sex-stratified GWAS for the female and male components, and a GWAS of chromosomal sex in the same participants for membership. **c**, Computational cost per additional variant on one core against sample size, for direct mixture maximum likelihood, G-MoQ Stages 1 and 2, and Stage 2 alone given the Stage 1 output. **d**,**e**, Two exemplar loci selected by a rule fixed in advance: rs1260326, with effects on all three parameters, and rs7097461, with a male-specific effect. Left, per-allele estimates with 95% confidence intervals; right, Stage 1 quantile effects (grey, 95% confidence intervals) with the fitted profile (black) and each parameter's contribution. Few participants lie between the modes, so effects near tau = 0.5 are very imprecise and values beyond the axis are drawn at its edge as triangles. Membership is plotted as -gamma, signed to match the GWAS of sex.

Source data: results/03_testosterone/recovery, results/03_testosterone/exemplars, results/03_testosterone/speed.
