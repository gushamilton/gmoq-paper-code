# fig6_heterogeneity_across_traits

Figure 6. Heterogeneity of genetic effects between lead variants across quantitative traits. 21 UK Biobank trait analyses (20 traits); 10,598 lead-variant entries across analyses, with leads per trait in brackets. **a**, Between-lead heterogeneity: the between-lead standard deviation of orthonormalised tilt per unit level, on the unit-standard-error basis with 95% bootstrap confidence intervals; grey symbols, the same estimate on the raw, log and Box-Cox scales. Colour, principal components above noise. Heterogeneity was tested by a one-sided Cochran Q test; every trait has P ≤ 1.4 × 10⁻⁴, not adjusted for multiple comparisons. **b**, Share of shape variance explained by the second principal component relative to its expectation if every lead shared one shape (parallel-analysis null median; tick, null 95th percentile), with 95% bootstrap intervals over leads (Methods); the dashed line at 1 marks the one-shape null median. **c**, Per-allele effects of six lead variants on log HbA1c, with pointwise 95% confidence intervals. Bottom, the three diabetes-associated leads with the largest asymmetry beyond shift and spread (diabetes, logistic regression in the same participants, two-sided P < 0.05/472); top, a lead with no asymmetry beyond the frame matched to each on the absolute mean-effect z statistic. Dashed grey lines show a fitted constant shift (top) and a fitted change in membership of the diabetes-enriched mixture component (bottom) (Methods).

Source data: results/06_traits/survey, results/06_traits/moments, results/06_traits/scale, results/06_traits/pc_heterogeneity, results/07_hba1c/skew_diabetes, results/07_hba1c/mixture.
