# fig4_bmi_models

Figure 4. Competing models of a genetic effect on BMI predict nearly the same quantile profile. n = 461,320. **a**, Analytic illustration on a log-normal baseline fitted to the Stage 1 BMI quantiles: non-carrier and carrier distributions (top) and their quantile difference (bottom) for an allele raising mean BMI by 2 kg/m2, exaggerated for display, under an additive shift, a proportional effect, a log-scale variance effect with the mean held fixed and a combined shift and proportional effect. **b**, Shift, proportional and combined quantile-effect profiles per unit mean effect (left), and pairwise angles for all four panel-a models in the whitened metric, with quantile-space angles in brackets (right). **c**, Stage 1 log-BMI profiles of 639 lead variants, each divided by its own mean effect (blue); the red line and band show the pointwise median and interquartile range. Reference curves show a shift on log BMI and a shift on raw BMI transported to log BMI. The simulation-calibrated, equal-rank comparison rejects a raw-scale additive shift at 570 of 639 leads (89.2%) on log BMI and the reverse comparison at one lead (Methods).
