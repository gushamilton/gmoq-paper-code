# fig4_bmi_models

Figure 4. Competing models of a genetic effect on BMI predict nearly the same quantile profile. n = 461,320. **a**, Analytic illustration on a log-normal baseline fitted to the Stage 1 BMI quantiles: non-carrier and carrier distributions (top) and their quantile difference (bottom) for an allele raising mean BMI by 2 kg/m2, exaggerated for display, under an additive shift, a multiplicative effect, a log-scale variance effect with the mean held fixed and a combined additive and multiplicative effect. **b**, The same models as quantile-effect profiles per unit mean effect (left) and the pairwise angles between them in the whitened metric, with the angle in quantile space in brackets (right). A raw-scale variance effect at constant coefficient of variation is exactly the multiplicative profile on this baseline. **c**, Stage 1 profiles of the 639 BMI lead variants, each divided by its own mean effect (blue); the red line and band are the pointwise median and interquartile range and the dashed line is a pure shift. Models are fitted to the same Stage 1 output by ordinary least squares with the pooled Stage 1 covariance; a purely additive profile is rejected in favour of a multiplicative one at 89% of leads (90% on the raw scale), and the reverse at one lead (Methods).

Source data: results/05_bmi/model_profiles, results/01_data/lead_profiles.
