# figS10_moments_pcs

Figure S10. Moment and principal-component descriptions of BMI lead-variant profiles. Log BMI; 639 lead variants (BMI-increasing allele), each Stage 1 profile divided by the lead's mean effect; n = 461,320. **a**, L-moment weight curves for shift (1), spread (2τ − 1) and skew (6τ² − 6τ + 1): the average of a profile multiplied by a curve gives that moment. **b**, Effect at τ = 0.89 against effect at τ = 0.11, each over the mean effect; each point is one lead. Blue dashed lines are the moment axes (pure shift through (1, 1) and pure spread orthogonal to it) and black lines the principal axes of the cloud; the first lies 18° above the shift axis. **c**, Share of shape variance explained by each principal component of the whitened unit-length profiles (log scale) against the 95th percentile of a parallel-analysis null (ticks); red, the 2 components above the null. **d**, What is left of each lead's unit-length profile after fitting PC1, PC1 + PC2, shift, and shift + spread (4.3%, 2.4%, 9.4%, 2.8% of total squared length; unwhitened, so not comparable with the whitened shares). The second component is compared across traits in Fig. 5b.

Source data: results/01_data/lead_profiles, results/06_traits/moments.
