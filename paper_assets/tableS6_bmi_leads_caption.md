# tableS6_bmi_leads

Table S6. Moments, polygenic-score interaction and model scores of the 639 BMI lead variants. One row per lead variant (effect allele, the BMI-increasing allele; n = 461,320). Moments of the Stage 1 quantile-effect profile on log BMI, with pooled-covariance standard errors (Methods): mean effect, change in variance, and changes in L-moment spread and skew; skew beyond shift and spread (z); lack of fit of the shift and spread frame (exact one-sided P, Davies' method, not adjusted for multiple comparisons); and the residual from the common tilt of the 639 leads (z). Polygenic-score interaction: regression of log BMI on the variant, a polygenic risk score excluding its chromosome (per s.d.), their product, age, sex and ten genetic principal components, with heteroskedasticity-robust standard errors; β is the main effect, δ the interaction and κᵢ = δ/β (noisy for single leads; common κ 0.119, s.e. 0.006; Fig. 6). Model scores, on raw and log BMI: AIC-like and BIC-like scores (full-covariance residual plus 2 or log n per parameter; Methods) of seven Stage 2 models: additive; log-additive (multiplicative about zero); shifted log-additive (about a fitted origin); additive + shifted log-additive; log-scale vQTL; additive + log-scale vQTL; and shift + spread + bend. Winner, runner-up and gap refer to the AIC-like score; the runner-up is within 2 units for 90% (raw) and 98% (log) of leads (Supplementary Fig. S13). AIC, Akaike information criterion; BIC, Bayesian information criterion; BMI, body mass index; s.d., standard deviation; s.e., standard error; vQTL, variance quantitative trait locus.

Source data: results/06_traits/moments, results/05_bmi/prs_interaction, results/05_bmi/model_scores.
