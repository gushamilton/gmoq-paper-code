# tableS4_antibody_leads

Table S4. Lead variants for JC polyomavirus and Merkel cell polyomavirus VP1 antibody levels. One row per lead variant and antigen; n = 9,145; log mean fluorescence intensity. Leads follow the rule used for every trait (REGENIE two-sided P < 5 × 10⁻⁸, clumped at r² < 0.01 within 10 Mb; Methods); Fig. 3 examples that are not leads are flagged ‘example, not a lead’. Stage 2 by ordinary least squares with the pooled Stage 1 covariance and mixture weights of the model design for JCV and the hybrid design for MCV (Supplementary Note 2); the alternative columns repeat γ, label, joint test and mixture Q under the other design. γ, lower location, upper location: per-allele effects (s.e.) on membership of the upper component (log odds) and on the lower- and upper-component locations. Joint test: −log₁₀ P of the Hotelling test of the three parameters. Mean / REGENIE: ratio of the mixture mean effect to the REGENIE effect. Label: ‘level’ if |z| < 1.96 for membership and for the component difference; ‘membership’ if |z| ≥ 5.45 for membership; otherwise ‘mixed’. Location L and spread S: exact location and scale coordinates (z). Detection bounds: smallest departure from the common spread-to-location line detectable at nominal and Bonferroni-corrected thresholds (%). Shift + spread lack of fit: residual of the profile from the location + scale frame (43 degrees of freedom), with exact P (Davies' method), printed ‘<1e-12’ below the computable floor. Mixture Q: residual from the fitted mixture (42 degrees of freedom). Membership is weakly identified: its scale rests on the kernel density near the gap, but its sign and z hardly depend on the design. Leads in the major histocompatibility complex (MHC, chromosome 6) are in strong linkage disequilibrium and are not independent signals. JCV, JC polyomavirus; MCV, Merkel cell polyomavirus; s.e., standard error; VP1, major capsid protein VP1.
