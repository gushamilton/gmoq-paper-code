# figS09_antibody_manhattan_qq

Figure S9. Genome-wide associations with JC and Merkel cell polyomavirus VP1 antibody levels. UK Biobank log MFI; n = 9,145 participants and 1,071,464 variants. G-MoQ Stage 2 is ordinary least squares with the pooled Stage 1 covariance (Methods). **a**, Manhattan plots of the G-MoQ joint Hotelling test of the three mixture parameters (above the axis) and REGENIE on log MFI (below), for MCV VP1 (top) and JCV VP1 (bottom). Grey points, the largest −log₁₀ P in each 1-Mb bin; coloured points, individual variants with joint P < 10⁻⁵, shown on both sides. Dashed lines, P = 5 × 10⁻⁸. **b**, Quantile–quantile plots of the joint test, the G-MoQ mean-effect test and REGENIE; each point is one quantile of the observed P-value distribution; dashed line, identity. Genomic inflation factors (λ, median): MCV VP1, joint 1.009, mean 1.020, REGENIE 1.020; JCV VP1, joint 1.000, mean 0.995, REGENIE 0.993. Counting loci within 500 kb, the joint and mean tests and REGENIE find the same 5 MCV VP1 and 4 JCV VP1 loci, with none unique to either method. P values are unadjusted for multiple comparisons. JCV, JC polyomavirus; MCV, Merkel cell polyomavirus; MFI, mean fluorescence intensity; VP1, viral capsid protein 1.

Source data: results/04_antibodies/decomposition.
