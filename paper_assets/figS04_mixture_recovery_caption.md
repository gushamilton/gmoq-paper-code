# figS04_mixture_recovery

Figure S4. G-MoQ Stage 2 and direct mixture maximum likelihood on simulated two-component mixtures. Parameters are the lower and upper component locations (δ_1, δ_2) and membership (γ), each against the method's own known truth; G-MoQ with pooled or 1,000-block jackknife covariance shares point estimates (Methods). Panels a and b use the same simulated datasets at three sample sizes: 20 replicate panels of 100 variants per sample size. **a**, Bias; point, mean over the panels; error bars, 95% Monte Carlo intervals (±1.96 RMSE/√20). **b**, Mean SE over the RMSE; the dashed line marks 1. **c**, Coverage of the nominal 95% confidence interval in a second simulation, by mixture design (weights fitted to the testosterone phenotype, unshrunk and shrunk, to JCV VP1 and to MCV VP1; S, component separation). Each design has 4 or 8 cells of sample size, minor allele frequency and effect, each of 200 replicates; point, mean over cells; bar, range over cells; grey band, 0.95 ± 1.96 times the median Monte Carlo standard error; dashed line, 0.95. **d**, Median time to fit one 100-variant panel with standard errors, over 3 panels per sample size: G-MoQ pooled 3.1 s against mixture maximum likelihood 48 s at 10,000 participants, and 53 s against 1,414 s at 400,000. No statistical tests are shown. ML, maximum likelihood; RMSE, root mean square error; SE, standard error; CI, confidence interval; JCV, JC polyomavirus; MCV, Merkel cell polyomavirus; VP1, major capsid protein VP1.

Source data: results/08_simulations/summary.
