Figure 7. Scale-invariant PC2 scores uncover distinct biological mechanisms across two-dimensional traits. UK Biobank lead variants; scale-invariant PC2 scores are computed from Stage 1 quantile-effect profiles blind to clinical diagnoses, environmental modifiers and medication use (Methods). **a**, ALT (316 lead variants; n = 439,662): scale-invariant PC2 score against ALT x BMI interaction z (Pearson r = 0.74, two-sided P = 3.8 x 10^-57), coloured by association with MASLD or chronic liver disease (13,882 cases; logistic regression, two-sided Bonferroni P < 0.05/316). Canonical fatty-liver loci (PNPLA3, TM6SF2, HSD17B13, GPAM, MBOAT7, TRIB1) populate the upper-right tail. **b**, Log urate (396 lead variants; n = 440,735; sex-adjusted in Stage 1): scale-invariant PC2 score against SNP x sex interaction z (Pearson r = 0.82, two-sided P = 2.9 x 10^-98), coloured by Bonferroni SNP x sex significance (two-sided P < 0.05/396); female-biased SLC2A9 variants separate cleanly from ABCG2. **c**, FEV1/FVC (421 lead variants; n = 345,527): scale-invariant PC2 score against SNP x ever-smoking interaction z (Pearson r = -0.27, two-sided P = 2.0 x 10^-8), coloured by Bonferroni SNP x ever-smoking significance (two-sided P < 0.05/421); the smoking-dependent CHRNA3/CHRNA5 locus (rs12914385; PC2 = -7.4, interaction z = 11.4) stands apart from all other variants. **d**, Per-allele quantile-effect profiles (95% confidence intervals) on LDL at APOE (rs7254892) and PCSK9 (rs11591147) in all participants (n = 440,443) and in statin-free participants (n = 364,354, excluding 76,089 cholesterol-lowering medication users). Statin treatment pulls high-LDL carriers into the lower tail, creating an inverted-U curve that flattens once statin users are excluded. ALT, alanine aminotransferase; BMI, body mass index; FEV1/FVC, forced expiratory volume in 1 s to forced vital capacity ratio; LDL, low-density lipoprotein cholesterol; MASLD, metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease.
