# figS07_weight_designs

Figure S7. Recovery of the membership effect under the model and hybrid weight designs. The model design divides every Stage 2 weight column by the fitted mixture density; the hybrid design divides the membership (γ) column by the Stage 1 KDE instead (Methods). **a**, Fitted mixture density over the Stage 1 KDE at 45 quantile levels for testosterone (n = 399,710), unshrunk and at three degrees of shrinkage; each line is one degree of shrinkage and the dashed line marks equality. At τ = 0.5 (points), between the modes, the ratios are 0.46 (unshrunk), 0.79 (0.2), 0.93 (0.5), 1.03 (0.7). **b**, Mean γ estimate over its true value for a variant affecting only membership, in nine simulated scenarios (400 replicates each; n = 100,000 unless stated), with 95% Monte Carlo confidence intervals; the right-hand column gives the coverage of nominal 95% confidence intervals under each design. The ratio is 0.35–0.92 under the model design and 0.97–1.00 under the hybrid design. In the 7 separated scenarios, the effect of a variant acting only on the narrower, upper component is overestimated by 2.2–5.9% under the hybrid design and 2.0–5.8% under the model design. KDE, kernel density estimate.

Source data: results/02_mixtures/stage2_mixtures, results/02_mixtures/weight_design/sim.
