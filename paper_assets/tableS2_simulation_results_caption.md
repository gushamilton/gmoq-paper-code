# tableS2_simulation_results

Table S2. Numerical results of the simulation studies. One row per study, scenario, sample size, minor allele frequency, parameter, method and covariance (design in Supplementary Table S1; Supplementary Note 1). Bias, RMSE, empirical s.d. (of the estimates over replicates) and mean SE are relative to the study's estimand; for study 3 the empirical s.d. is taken across variants with different true effects and is not an error s.d. Coverage is of nominal 95% confidence intervals, with its Monte Carlo standard error. Attempted, succeeded and failed count replicates (variants in study 3, coefficients in study 1). SE agreement is the ratio of G-MoQ to ordinary RIF–OLS standard errors (study 1) or, for study 7, the median ratio of pooled to jackknife standard errors in the empirical fit, taken over the variants of the stated chromosomes (testosterone with model-design weights; JC and Merkel cell polyomavirus VP1 with the weights of the 44-antigen scan); the coverage column of study 7 gives the range of null coverage for each covariance over that case's simulated cells (200 replicates each). Timing columns give the time to fit with standard errors and the whole-process wall time (s) and peak resident memory (MiB), as means of the stated repeats; mixture timing rows give the median over three 100-variant panels for G-MoQ with pooled covariance against direct mixture maximum likelihood. MAF, minor allele frequency; MiB, mebibyte; ML, maximum likelihood; RIF–OLS, recentred influence function ordinary least squares; RMSE, root mean square error; s.d., standard deviation; SE, standard error.

Source data: results/08_simulations/summary.
