# tableS5_trait_survey_scale

Table S5. Trait survey and scale. One row per trait: the 21 traits of Fig. 5 (19 survey traits, log BMI and height-adjusted weight), on the analysed scale; Box–Cox powers refer to the raw trait (raw BMI for log BMI). Leads: lead variants. Components: principal components of the whitened lead profiles above the parallel-analysis null. Skew leads, raw: leads with a third L-moment effect; skew-beyond leads: leads with skew beyond the shift and spread frame (Bonferroni within trait, analysed scale). λ normalising: Box–Cox power that best normalises the trait (maximum normal probability-plot correlation over 193 quantile levels, searched from −5 to 5 in steps of 0.01; ‘at search edge’ flags the limit of the search). λ ML: maximum-likelihood power with 95% profile interval (undefined when any value is ≤ 0). λ flat, pooled: Box–Cox power closest to the implied natural scale, under which the leads' common profile is a pure shift. Strong leads: leads with |mean z| > 10 on the raw scale; λ flat per lead: median and interquartile range over strong leads of the power at which a lead's converted profile has no L-moment spread. Spread leads, components and skew-beyond leads by scale: counts with every lead converted exactly to the Box–Cox, log and raw scales (Bonferroni within trait). Component counts do not depend on the scale, because a change of scale rotates all whitened profiles together; spread counts do (BMI 152 raw, 51 log, 11 Box–Cox). BMI, body mass index; IQR, interquartile range; ML, maximum likelihood.

Source data: results/06_traits/survey, results/06_traits/scale.
