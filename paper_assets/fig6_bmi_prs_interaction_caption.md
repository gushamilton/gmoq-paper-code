# fig6_bmi_prs_interaction

Figure 6. BMI variant effects grow with polygenic background. Log BMI, 639 lead variants aligned to the BMI-increasing allele; n = 461,320. For each lead, log BMI was regressed on the variant, a polygenic score excluding that variant's chromosome, their interaction, age, sex and ten genetic principal components, with heteroscedasticity-robust standard errors. **a**, Distribution of the interaction z statistic against the standard normal null; interactions are positive at 81% of leads. **b**, Interaction against main effect per lead, with 95% confidence intervals; the red line is the common relative interaction kappa = 0.119 (95% confidence interval 0.109 to 0.130). **c**, Effect of the FTO lead rs1421085 on log BMI by decile of the polygenic score, with 95% confidence intervals; the top decile effect is 1.61 times the bottom (95% confidence interval 1.26 to 2.05). **d**, The common tilt of the lead profiles, observed and as predicted if each allele's effect grew with a modifier correlated with BMI, for four candidate modifiers. Amplification by polygenic background is one reading of these results; a convex measurement scale and environments correlated with genetic liability predict the same pattern.

Source data: results/05_bmi/prs_interaction.
