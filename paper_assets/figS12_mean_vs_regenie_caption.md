# figS12_mean_vs_regenie

Figure S12. Agreement of the Stage 1 mean effect with the REGENIE effect. The Stage 1 mean effect (W = 1) is the unweighted average of the 45 quantile effects; REGENIE was run in the same participants with the same covariates (Methods). **a**, Each point is a variant: 5,000 randomly sampled variants (grey) and every variant with REGENIE P < 10⁻⁵ (blue; BMI 31,911, log BMI 33,226, weight 31,875, JCV VP1 205, MCV VP1 237). Dashed line, identity; r, Pearson correlation over all tested variants (1,200,240 for BMI and weight traits, 1,071,464 for VP1). **b**, Each pair of points is one trait: the least-squares slope of the Stage 1 mean effect on the REGENIE effect over all variants (circles) and over variants with REGENIE P < 5 × 10⁻⁸ (triangles; grey segment joins the two), against the median ratio of the two standard errors; testosterone is shown at three shrinkage settings (Methods). Dashed line, identity. All-variant slopes (0.971–0.997) follow the SE ratio (0.984–1.001; JCV VP1 slope 0.971, SE ratio 0.984); slopes at P < 5 × 10⁻⁸ are 0.988–1.025. Unshrunk testosterone (not shown) has an all-variant slope of 1.18 with SE ratio 1.20, and a slope of 0.980 at P < 5 × 10⁻⁸. BMI, body mass index; JCV, JC polyomavirus; MCV, Merkel cell polyomavirus; SE, standard error; VP1, viral capsid protein 1.
