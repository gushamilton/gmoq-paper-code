Figure 3. Decomposition of antibody associations into membership and level effects. UK Biobank JC and Merkel cell polyomavirus VP1 IgG, log mean fluorescence intensity; n = 9,145. **a**, Phenotype histograms with the fitted two-component skew-normal mixture that defines the Stage 2 weights: total density (mixture) and the lower and upper components. MCV VP1 is bimodal, so its components can be read as seronegative-like and seropositive-like groups; JCV VP1 is unimodal and its components describe shape rather than exposure groups. **b**-**d**, Three lead variants: JCV VP1 at HLA (rs9271055), JCV VP1 at FUT2 (rs681343) and MCV VP1 at HLA (rs9271055). Left, per-allele effects with 95% confidence intervals from REGENIE on all-sample log MFI, on assay-defined seropositivity (log odds) and on IgG among seropositive participants, beside the G-MoQ mean and the three mixture parameters: lower-component mean, upper-component mean and the log odds of membership of the upper component. Right, Stage 1 quantile effects (grey, 95% confidence intervals) with the fitted profile (black) and each parameter's contribution. At JCV, HLA acts mainly through membership, and at MCV it also moves both component means; FUT2 shifts both component means with little effect on membership. Stage 2 is ordinary least squares with the pooled Stage 1 covariance (Methods). MFI, mean fluorescence intensity.

Source data: results/04_antibodies/decomposition, results/04_antibodies/serostatus_gwas.
