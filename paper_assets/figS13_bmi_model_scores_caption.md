# figS13_bmi_model_scores

Figure S13. Per-lead model selection for BMI lead variants. Each of the 639 BMI lead variants was fitted on the log scale with seven models of its Stage 1 quantile-effect profile, by ordinary least squares with the pooled Stage 1 covariance, and scored by an AIC-like criterion using the full covariance (Methods). **a**, Per winning model: the number of leads it wins (Wins) and their share (%); the median AIC-like gap from the winner to the runner-up (Median gap); the percentage of its wins with a gap below 2 (Gap < 2); and the percentage of its wins whose residual lack-of-fit test rejects at P < 0.05 (Lack of fit; one-sided χ² test on 45 − k degrees of freedom, k the model's effective number of parameters; not adjusted for multiple comparisons). Top row, all leads. **b**, Distribution of the gap across the 639 leads; bars, leads per 0.1-unit bin; dashed line, a gap of 2. 98.9% of leads (632 of 639) have a runner-up within 2 units, so the winning model need not identify the mechanism (Fig. 4b); near-ties mostly pair a model with its own one-parameter extension. AIC, Akaike information criterion; BMI, body mass index; vQTL, variance quantitative trait locus.

Source data: results/05_bmi/model_scores.
