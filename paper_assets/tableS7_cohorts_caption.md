# tableS7_cohorts

Table S7. Cohorts and variant counts for all traits with a Stage 1 quantile GWAS. One row per trait and scale (log-transformed traits are marked ‘(log)’). N (sample): participants in the analysis sample with the trait; N phenotyped: with a valid phenotype; N after LOCO: with leave-one-chromosome-out predictions, where used; N analysed: the final number analysed. Covariates: the covariates, with their number in brackets; weight is the height-adjusted model (sex, age, four height terms and genetic principal components 1–10). Variants: HapMap3 variants analysed (minor allele frequency above 0.05 additionally required for the antibodies). Leads: lead variants (REGENIE where available, otherwise the Stage 1 mean effect; Methods); antibody leads are those of a uniform REGENIE clump (JCV VP1 3, MCV VP1 4), and the testosterone analyses use sex-stratified lead sets (Methods). Use: where the trait is reported; lipoprotein(a) was analysed but is excluded from the paper because its measurements are censored at both assay limits, and traits marked ‘Not reported’ were analysed but are not used. JCV, JC polyomavirus; LOCO, leave-one-chromosome-out; MCV, Merkel cell polyomavirus; PC, genetic principal component; VP1, major capsid protein VP1.

Source data: results/01_data/sample_sizes, results/01_data/stage1_gwas_index, results/01_data/lead_variants, results/06_traits/survey.
